About Me

I'm Jaysen Sordum Barikpoa, a technology professional, writer, product builder, and digital strategist interested in how technology can make work simpler, businesses stronger, and ideas easier to bring to life.

My background spans IT support, project and product management, research, digital marketing, content development, and customer experience. Working across these areas has taught me to look beyond individual tools and focus on the bigger picture: understanding a problem, finding a practical solution, and turning that solution into something people can actually use.

I have a particular interest in artificial intelligence and its practical application in business and everyday work. I write, experiment with AI tools, develop digital products, and explore ways individuals and organisations can use technology to work more intelligently without losing the human thinking behind the work.

I'm also the founder of Eztec Systems, where I work on technology products designed to solve practical problems for businesses and professionals across Africa.

My approach is simple: understand the problem, think clearly, use technology intelligently, and build things that are genuinely useful.

Through my work, writing, and projects, I hope to help more people understand technology not as something complicated or distant, but as a practical tool for creating better work, better businesses, and better opportunities.

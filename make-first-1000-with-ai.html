PROMPT ARCHITECTURE

The POWER Framework

The 5-part prompt engineering method that drives every business task in this book.

P

Purpose

What you are asking AI to produce. One clear, unambiguous business task stated plainly so AI answers the exact question.

O

Output

The exact format, word count, structure, and tone. Ensures responses arrive as actionable tables, JSON, or copy rather than generic essays.

W

Who

The precise role, perspective, and domain expertise AI must adopt. Replaces generic advice with culturally attuned, industry-specific insight.

E

Examples

The specific ground-level context, constraints, customer quotes, and data that anchor the AI’s output to your actual market.

R

Refine

The self-correcting instruction forcing the model to critique and polish its own draft from the perspective of a skeptical buyer before delivering it.
